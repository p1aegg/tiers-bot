About Name Tiers

Name Tiers ranks the best Minecraft PvP players across multiple game modes. Tiers go from Tier 1 (best) downward, split into HT (high tier) and LT (low tier). Players earn points based on their position in each mode. Click any player to open their full profile, and hover any tier badge to see the game mode it belongs to.
